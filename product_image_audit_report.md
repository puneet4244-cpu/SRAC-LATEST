# Shree Ram & Company – Vieta Stone
## Product Visual Content & Watermarking Audit Report

### Executive Summary
This audit confirms that all product cards and category pages across the Shree Ram & Company (Vieta Stone) website have been updated strictly according to the **IMAGE-ONLY** scope:
- **No changes** to layout, design, colours, fonts, text, spacing, animations, routes, or code logic.
- **No products, categories, or cards** added, renamed, or removed.
- **Card image counts strictly preserved:** Double Height Wall remains a 6-image carousel; all other standalone category product cards remain 5-image carousels; home page collection cards remain 1 image each.
- **Material Accuracy (100% Strict):** 
  - Authentic Makrana / Indian & Vietnam white marble used exclusively for marble products with natural veining.
  - Authentic High-Density Moisture-Resistant (HDMR) / MDF boards with CNC-routed grooves and matte PU/teak finishes used for MDF products (strictly NO stone).
  - Authentic Indian sandstones (Gwalior Mint, Red Karauli, Pink Bansi Paharpur, Jaisalmer Yellow, Teak Sandstone, Kandla Grey, Moka Grey) used for all stone carving, wall panels, temples, and elevation.
  - Authentic weatherproof WPC polymer for PVC/WPC jali screens.
  - Active flowing water for water fountains.
  - Accurate, respectful iconography for all deity murals.
- **Burned-in Bottom-Right Watermark:** Every product image has the professional watermark stamped in the image file itself:
  - **Text:** `Shree Ram & Company Vieta Stone`
  - **Position:** Bottom-right corner (~3% margin from right and bottom edges)
  - **Style:** Clean elegant serif font (Georgia), white text at ~60% opacity with a subtle soft dark shadow for high legibility across both dark and light stone backgrounds without covering main carvings.
- **Unwatermarked Backup:** Complete backup of all original, unwatermarked high-resolution images preserved in `/assets/originals-unwatermarked/`.
- **Descriptive Alt Text:** Comprehensive, material-specific descriptive `alt` attributes applied to each image tag across all pages.
