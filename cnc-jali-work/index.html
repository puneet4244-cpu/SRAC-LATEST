Precision CNC Jali Design: From CAD Blueprint to Installation

CNC jali design represents the ideal union of Rajasthan’s heritage architectural perforated lattice (mashrabiya) and industrial 3-axis, 4-axis, and 5-axis CNC machinery. Unlike handmade manual piercings which can deviate across repetitive panels, our computer-controlled CNC routers guarantee flawless mathematical symmetry, continuous geometric alignment, and crisp edge bevels.

Whether you select timeless Mughal floral motifs, intricate Islamic geometric stars, or sleek modern linear diagonals, our Jaipur factory manufactures panels to your exact wall framing or structural aperture dimensions. We supply direct to architects, luxury homeowners, and interior designers across India and worldwide, complete with pre-drilled hardware channels and custom mounting borders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key architectural, material, and logistical considerations for custom CNC jali orders.

We manufacture CNC jali screens across four primary materials: natural Rajasthan stone (sandstone and marble for facades and mandirs), high-density moisture-resistant MDF/HDMR (for painted interior partitions and pooja rooms), engineered partition composites, and waterproof/termite-proof WPC (for outdoor balconies and wet areas).

Stone jali cutting requires heavy-duty multi-axis industrial CNC milling machines operating with continuous water-coolant diamond drill bits to slice through dense sandstone slabs up to 50mm thick. MDF and WPC jali use high-speed tungsten carbide router bits for crisp geometric cutouts.

Yes. Our Jaipur drafting studio accepts AutoCAD (.dwg/.dxf), Illustrator (.ai/.eps), and PDF vector files. If you only have a reference photo, our in-house drafters convert it into a production-ready vector blueprint for your approval before machining.

For interior MDF and WPC jali, thicknesses range from 12mm to 25mm. For architectural natural stone jali, standard thicknesses range from 25mm to 50mm (1 inch to 2 inches) depending on panel height and structural wind-load requirements.

Production typically takes 1 to 3 weeks depending on the material, total square footage, and cutting intricacy. All orders are carefully packed in wooden crates and dispatched across India with transit insurance.
