Our Atelier Story

Bespoke Stone Artistry from the Heart of Jaipur

Established in Jaipur, Rajasthan—the historic epicenter of Indian architectural stonemasonry—Shree Ram & Company (operating under the mark Vijeta Stone) represents over four decades of unbroken generational mastery in natural stone sculpting, temple architecture, and precision architectural fabrication.

Our journey began with a singular devotion: preserving the ancient stone chiseling traditions of Rajasthan while elevating them to meet modern architectural precision. In our Govindpuri atelier, generational stone carvers whose ancestors contributed to Rajasthan’s royal palaces and monumental temples work side-by-side with modern CAD engineers. Every raw block of Bansi Paharpur pink sandstone, Gwalior mint sandstone, and Makrana pure white marble is carefully examined for mineral grain, structural density, and chromatic harmony before a single chisel touches its surface.

Today, our bespoke creations adorn luxury villas, private farmhouses, corporate headquarters, and sacred spiritual mandirs across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and overseas estates in the United States, the United Kingdom, and the United Arab Emirates.

From Quarry to Installation

Our Four-Stage Sculpting Protocol

How raw geological blocks are transformed into millimeter-accurate architectural stone art.

01

Certified Quarry Sourcing

Direct ethical procurement from certified quarries in Bansi Paharpur, Makrana, and Dholpur. Blocks are ultrasound-tested to eliminate hidden fault lines.

02

Multi-Axis CNC Roughing

Precision multi-axis CNC routers carve initial 3D geometries, dimensional bevels, and structural interlocking grooves down to 0.5mm tolerances.

03

Master Hand-Chiseling

Generational sculptors detail facial expressions, floral relief depths (25mm–75mm), and tactile surface textures that machines cannot replicate.

04

Dry-Fit Pre-Assembly

Every multi-panel wall, temple, or gazebo is fully dry-assembled in our Jaipur yard to verify joint tightness, plumbness, and anchor alignment before dispatch.
