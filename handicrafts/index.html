Buyer's Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What materials are used for Handicrafts by Shree Ram & Company?

Our Handicrafts creations are crafted using authentic Rajasthan natural stones including Gwalior Mint Sandstone, Pink Sandstone Bansi Paharpur, Teak Sandstone, Jaisalmer Sandstone, Makrana White Marble, and premium engineered substrates like HDMR for interior paneling or waterproof WPC/PVC for outdoor jali installations.

Q. Can Handicrafts be customized for specific site dimensions and architectural drawings?

Yes, 100% of our Handicrafts orders are custom sculpted to your exact site blueprints, ceiling height, and CAD designs. Our Jaipur atelier handles everything from initial architectural shop drawings to final stone carving.

Q. How are Handicrafts panels installed on interior or exterior walls?

Panels are engineered with interlocking tongue-and-groove joints, SS mechanical dry-hanging anchors, or high-strength epoxy adhesive backings, ensuring seamless alignment and structural stability across single and double-height walls.

Q. What is the typical production timeline for Handicrafts order from Jaipur?

Standard hand-carving and precision CNC manufacturing takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on order volume and detail complexity. Complete pan-India door delivery and onsite installation guidance are provided.

Q. How do I maintain and clean Handicrafts over time?

Natural stone surfaces require minimal maintenance. Periodic dry dusting and washing with neutral pH soap water keep stone glowing. Exterior stone surfaces receive breathable hydrophobic sealants that protect against water staining and weathering.
