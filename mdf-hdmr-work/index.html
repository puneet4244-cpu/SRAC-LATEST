HDMR Millwork Buyer's Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is HDMR board and why is it superior to standard MDF for interior wall paneling?

HDMR stands for High Density Moisture Resistant board. Unlike standard MDF (which has a density around 650–700 kg/m³ and swells when exposed to humidity), HDMR is engineered with a density exceeding 850–900 kg/m³ using specialized synthetic resin binders. This higher fiber density gives HDMR superior screw-holding strength, excellent moisture resistance in air-conditioned environments, and clean, fuzz-free edges during detailed CNC routing that achieve a flawless automotive-grade PU paint finish.

Q. How are 3D CNC fluted HDMR wall panels mounted securely on interior masonry walls?

HDMR wall panels are mounted using an interlocking French cleat or aluminum Z-clip sub-frame system. A calibrated plywood or wooden framework (12mm–18mm) is first anchored to the brick or concrete wall using nylon expansion fasteners, creating an air gap that prevents moisture transfer from the masonry. The pre-finished CNC HDMR panels, fitted with matching reverse cleats on their back, lock securely into the sub-frame without any visible screw holes or nails on the decorative front face.

Q. What paint and polishing options work best on CNC carved HDMR surfaces?

The ideal finish for CNC carved HDMR is multi-coat Polyurethane (PU) paint applied in a controlled dust-free spray booth. The process involves two coats of high-build polyester/PU primer to seal the routed fibers, thorough sanding to 400-grit smoothness, followed by two coats of premium Italian PU topcoat (available in deep matte, satin 20% sheen, or 100% high-gloss buffed finish). PU coatings provide a waterproof, scratch-resistant, and non-yellowing surface that retains its luster for decades.

Q. What is the cost per square foot for custom CNC 3D HDMR wall paneling?

The cost of custom CNC 3D HDMR wall paneling typically ranges between Rs 280 to Rs 850 per square foot in India. Standard 2D fluted or reeded patterns in 12mm–18mm HDMR board (unpainted or primer-coated) range from Rs 280 to Rs 450 per sq ft. Complex 3D organic wave panels or geometric parametric designs with professional spray-booth PU duco finish range from Rs 550 to Rs 950+ per sq ft, depending on panel thickness and finish specification.
