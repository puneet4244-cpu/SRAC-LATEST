=== PRODUCT IMAGES UPLOAD DIRECTORY ===

Aap apni photos ko is folder me paste kar sakte hain:

1_Wall_Surfaces/
   - Stone Carving (Double Height Wall, Staircase Wall, Sofa Wall, Statement Wall, etc.)
   - Stone Art & Murals (Radha Krishna, Buddha, Hanuman Ji, Durga Mata Ji, Ganesh Ji, etc.)
   - Stone Wall Panels (Fluted, Textured, Wave, Geometrical)
   - MDF HDMR Work

2_Exterior_Elevation/
   - Elevation Facade, Customised Name Plates, Wall Cladding, Garden Articles

3_Temples_and_Statues/
   - Marble Temples, Stone Temple, Pooja Rooms, Marble Inlay, Statues, Gazebos, Arches & Mehrabs, Pillars

4_Home_Interior_and_Decor/
   - Handicrafts, Marble Table Tops, Indoor Fountains

5_CNC_and_Jali_Work/
   - Stone Jali, MDF Jali, WPC Jali, Partition Jali

TIP:
- Aap photo ka file name product ke name se milta-julta rakh sakte hain (jaise "radha-krishna-1.jpg", "marble-temple.jpg", "indoor-fountain.jpg").
- Photo paste karne ke baad hume chat me bas bata dein, hum automatically:
  1. 900x1200 (3:4 ratio) me center-crop/fit karenge
  2. Studio color grading & texture contrast boost karenge
  3. Website pages aur cards par link kar denge!
