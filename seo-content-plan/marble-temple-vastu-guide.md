## 3. Structural Dimensions & Architectural Heights
1. **Pedestal Elevation (Chowki Height):**  
   The base of the temple Garbhagriha must be elevated at least 15 to 24 inches above the floor level. When seated or standing during prayers, the idols should be situated directly between your chest and eye level.
2. **Idol Height Regulations:**  
   For household mandirs, idol heights should ideally measure between 6 inches and 12 inches. Shilpa Shastra cautions against placing murtis exceeding 15 inches inside home shrines without formal pran-pratishtha daily temple rituals.
3. **The Shikhara (Dome Spire):**  
   A pyramidal or traditional domed Shikhara with a brass or carved stone Kalash at the apex acts as a spiritual antenna, concentrating prana throughout the residence.

## 4. Selection of Marble: Why Makrana White Marble Excels
* **Zero Chemical Reactivity:** Daily abhishek with milk, honey, gangajal, and rosewater will not erode or stain authentic Makrana marble.
* **Non-Yellowing:** While imported soft marbles turn yellowish within 3–5 years from diya lamp heat, Makrana marble retains its radiant crystalline white translucence for generations.
* **Thermal Insulation:** Marble keeps idols cool, preserving the divine sanctum's tranquil microclimate.

---

## 5. Things to Avoid in Pooja Room Architecture
* Never build a marble temple sharing a common wall with a bathroom, toilet, or beneath a staircase.
* Never place a mandir inside the master bedroom; if unavoidable in compact apartments, ensure a carved marble or wood jali curtain can be closed at night.
* Avoid damaged or broken idols (Khandit Murtis).
