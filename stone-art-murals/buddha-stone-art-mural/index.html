Mindful Iconography & Zen Sculpture

Timeless Serenity: Lord Buddha in Hand-Carved Stone Relief

A masterfully sculpted Buddha stone mural transforms residential and wellness interiors into tranquil sanctuaries of contemplation. Our Jaipur sculptors carve Lord Buddha seated in classical Padmasana upon a blooming multi-layered lotus pedestal, displaying the grounding Bhumisparsha mudra (earth-touching gesture) or meditative Dhyana mudra beneath the protective canopy of the Bodhi tree.

Each relief is carved with subtle contoured planar gradations (30mm to 70mm relief depth) that capture the serene oval countenance, elongated earlobes symbolizing spiritual renunciation, curling ushnisha cranial protuberance, and translucent monastic sanghati drapery. Chiseled from solid slabs of fine-grain sandstone or crystalline white marble, these non-sectarian spiritual compositions harmonize effortlessly with modern minimalist, Japandi, and luxury neoclassical architecture.
