Heroic Strength & Humble Bhakti Embodied in Natural Stone

Lord Hanuman represents the highest synthesis of invincible strength (Veera) and selfless devotion (Bhakti). In our bespoke stone wall reliefs, our master sculptors capture Bajrang Bali either in his heroic flying posture carrying the sacred Dronagiri mountain with Sanjeevani herbs, wielding his mighty golden mace (Gada), or seated in peaceful Anjali mudra devotion before Prabhu Shri Ram.

Every anatomical contour, the dynamic movement of his tail (Langula), the flying drapery, and his resolute yet compassionate expression are sculpted with relief depths of 30mm to 70mm. Handcrafted from historic Bansi Paharpur pink sandstone, Red Agra stone, or Indian white marble at our Jaipur factory, these monumental murals instill an aura of fearlessness and protection.

Architectural Guidelines for Bajrang Bali Wall Reliefs

Monumental Dimensions, Heroic Stone Selection & Altar Mounting

Hanuman stone murals demand robust structural backing and precise orientation to capture their heroic energy and devotional presence.

Curated Size Configurations

  • Private Pooja Niche: 3 ft x 4.5 ft (35mm relief depth, single-slab portrait)
  • Courtyard & Fitness Sanctuary Elevation: 5 ft x 7 ft (50mm relief depth, dynamic Veer posture)
  • Monumental Boundary Wall / Temple Facade: 8 ft x 11 ft (65mm relief depth across interlocking slabs)

Recommended Stone Selections

  • Bansi Paharpur Pink Sandstone: Sacred pink stone embodying the heroic Veer rasa and historic temple sanctity
  • Agra Red Sandstone: Deep terra-cotta red stone projecting commanding terrestrial power and rustic warmth
  • Pure Indian White Marble: Pristine crystalline stone chosen for quiet, devotional Anjali mudra pooja rooms

Mounting & Lighting Protocol

  • Heavy-duty Grade-304 stainless steel anchor bolts with high-strength structural anchoring epoxy
  • Traditionally oriented on the South or South-West wall in accordance with traditional Hanuman upasana principles
  • Directional 3500K neutral-warm spotlighting casting dramatic shadows across the muscular contours and mountain
