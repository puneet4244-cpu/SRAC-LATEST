Divine Pushti Bhakti: Shri Shreenath Ji in Hand-Carved Stone Relief

Shreenath Ji is the seven-year-old childhood manifestation (Balak) of Shri Krishna as Govardhandhara, celebrated across the Pushtimarg tradition founded by Vallabhacharya. In this deeply reverent composition, our Jaipur sculptors depict Shreenath Ji lifting Mount Govardhan with his left hand held high, his right fist resting gently upon his waist, and his gaze downward bestowing unconditional grace upon loving devotees.

Our sculptors pay meticulous attention to the distinctive Pichwai-style iconography: the large almond-shaped kamalnayan eyes, the tender Mukharvind facial features, the sacred diamond (heera) upon the chin, the layered floral vanamala garlands, and surrounding Surabhi cows. Sculpted in deep dimensional relief (30mm to 65mm) from solid Rajasthan sandstone or dense white marble, these murals evoke the sanctity of a Nathdwara Haveli in private pooja rooms and dining foyers.
