Statement Stone Carving Wall

Request Custom Quotation WhatsApp Inquiry
Master Artisanship & Impact

Architectural Elegance That Redefines Vertical Space

A statement wall is designed to do one thing unequivocally: captivate every gaze and define the entire identity of a space. Standard wall treatments like paint, textured plaster, or imported tiles lack the monumental presence and authentic substance required for luxury hotel lobbies, corporate headquarters, and palatial private estates. Our handcrafted statement stone carving walls transform ordinary partition boundaries into monumental sculptural masterworks. Sculpted in our Jaipur atelier by Sompura artisans with over 40 years of royal heritage experience, these walls feature bold high-relief undercuts, dramatic multi-layered geometric tessellations, and sweeping organic waveforms up to 75mm deep. Sourced directly from certified Rajasthan quarries in Makrana, Bansi Paharpur, and Gwalior, each slab is hand-matched for continuous mineral veining. When grazed with precision architectural lighting, the interplay of sharp ridges and deep shadows creates an unforgettable theatrical atmosphere of timeless authority and cultural majesty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on pricing, mechanical dry-cladding installation, and custom lead times.

A Statement Wall features dramatic high-relief sculpting (often 40mm to 75mm deep), custom-engineered continuous motifs across monumental expanses, and integrated mechanical dry-hanging systems designed to serve as the defining visual anchor of an entire property.

Statement walls range between ₹650 and ₹2,400+ per sq. ft., depending on the chosen natural stone, carving relief depth, and structural engineering requirements. We provide complete itemized cost sheets covering raw material, 3D carving, packaging, and transit.

Yes. Our computer-aided design team converts high-resolution vector files, corporate emblems, or custom artistic sketches into multi-axis CNC toolpaths, which our master carvers subsequently refine by hand to achieve organic, sculptural depth.

We conduct structural weight calculations and supply calibrated SS-304/316 dry-cladding brackets, anchor bolts, and interlocking stone rebates. This transfers the entire physical load directly to the building's structural framework, ensuring safety even in seismic zones.

Due to the scale and intensive multi-stage hand finishing, fabrication generally requires 3 to 5 weeks depending on total square footage. We provide weekly photographic progress reports directly from our Jaipur carving atelier.
