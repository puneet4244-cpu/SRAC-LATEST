Master Artisanship & Impact

Architectural Elegance That Redefines Vertical Space

A double height wall is the defining architectural statement of modern luxury duplexes, penthouses, and palatial villas. Spanning 18 to 28 feet in vertical grandeur, standard paint or wallpaper fails to hold the visual weight required by such monumental volume. Our handcrafted double height stone carving walls turn these colossal expanses into breathtaking vertical galleries of tactile artistry. Combining cutting-edge multi-axis CNC profiling with traditional hand-chiseling, each monolithic panel is calibrated to perfection, creating seamless flowing waves, radial mandalas, or sculptural floral motifs that ascend toward the ceiling. The natural mineral veins and tactile relief depth capture changing daylight and architectural grazing illumination, imbuing grand living rooms, entrance foyers, and atrium lobbies with an enduring sense of majesty rooted in Rajasthan's royal heritage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on pricing, mechanical dry-cladding installation, and custom lead times.

Pricing depends on natural stone selection (indigenous Rajasthan sandstone vs premium white marble), relief carving depth (bas-relief vs deep 3D sculptural undercut), and total wall square footage. Contact our Jaipur factory with your architectural drawings or wall measurements for an itemized quotation.

Safety and structural integrity are paramount for tall installations. We utilize engineered SS-304/SS-316 stainless steel dry-hanging clamps, anchor fasteners, and epoxy interlocking tongue-and-groove joints. This mechanical dry-cladding system distributes weight directly to the structural RCC wall without relying solely on adhesive mortars.

Yes, 100%. We work directly from CAD drawings, Pinterest mood boards, interior renders, or sketch concepts. Our design studio generates precise 2D/3D shop drawings showing exact tile junctions, lighting conduits, and scale proportions before carving begins.

For warm, regal interior ambiance, Dholpur Pink and Gwalior Mint sandstones are exceptionally popular due to their fine grain, acoustic properties, and soft light reflection. For ultra-contemporary or pristine spiritual aesthetics, Makrana White Marble or Bansi Paharpur sandstone provides an unmatched luxurious sheen.

Production timelines depend on total wall area, architectural drawing complexity, and relief carving depth. Panels are roughed using industrial 3-axis, 4-axis, and 5-axis CNC routers, followed by detailed hand-carving by experienced artisans. Finished slabs are numbered sequentially, packed securely on wooden pallets or crates with protective foam or chemical foam safe packing, and delivered directly to your project site across India and worldwide.

Breathable stone sealers can be applied upon request to protect the stone surface. General maintenance requires only periodic dry dusting using an electrostatic micro-fiber duster or a soft-bristle vacuum brush. No harsh chemical detergents or abrasive cleaners are needed.
