Master Artisanship & Impact

Architectural Elegance That Redefines Vertical Space

The sofa wall is the primary visual anchor of any formal living room or salon. It is the backdrop against which all family gatherings, luxury entertainment, and interior aesthetics unfold. Flat paint or temporary laminate paneling quickly loses its charm and lacks the physical presence required to complement bespoke upholstery and Italian furnishings. Our handcrafted sofa backdrop stone carving walls provide an authentic architectural foundation of enduring luxury. Sourced from Rajasthan's royal quarries and sculpted in our Jaipur workshop, these tactile panels feature rhythmic organic wave ripples, delicate lotus medallions, or classical geometric jali relief. Calibrated to align harmoniously with sofa back heights, wall sconces, and ceiling coves, the three-dimensional stone surface interacts warmly with ambient lighting, casting soft, soothing shadows. Naturally fireproof, sound-dampening, and effortless to maintain, a natural stone sofa wall establishes an atmosphere of grounded elegance and royal Rajasthani refinement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on pricing, mechanical dry-cladding installation, and custom lead times.

Typically, sofa wall installations range from ₹450 to ₹1,600 per square foot depending on stone material, relief complexity, and factory finish. A typical 12x9 ft living room sofa wall ranges from ₹50,000 to ₹1,75,000 complete with pre-engineered mounting hardware.

We generally recommend starting panel cladding either from the skirting board (floor level) or 6 inches below the top of the sofa back, extending up to the ceiling coving or picture rail (typically 8 to 11 feet high) to provide a complete, unbroken architectural frame.

Yes, absolutely. We incorporate electrical backbox cutouts and concealed vertical cable conduits during fabrication at our Jaipur workshop. When installed on site, electricians simply pull wires through pre-aligned junction channels without damaging the carved surface.

Because our panels are factory-impregnated with breathable silane-siloxane sealers, dust particles sit lightly on the surface rather than bonding into the pores. A weekly light pass with an electrostatic duster or a soft brush vacuum cleans the grooves in under two minutes.

For walls under 10 feet in standard residential interiors, high-bond polymer modified epoxies (such as Laticrete or MYK Laticrete) combined with discreet bottom support angles provide superior, permanent adhesion. For taller walls or heavier marble slabs, we provide mechanical SS Z-brackets.

Panels are individually wrapped in protective foam sheets, packed tightly in export-standard wooden crates lined with moisture barriers, and dispatched via air-suspension logistics with complete transit insurance to your project address.
