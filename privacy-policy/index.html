Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At Shree Ram & Company (operating under the trade name Vijeta Stone), based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, we value the trust of our clients, architects, interior designers, and luxury homeowners. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information when you browse our website or submit project inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us for bespoke stone carving consultations, quote estimates, or 3D architectural drawings, we collect information provided voluntarily by you, including:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number and WhatsApp contact details
  • Project city and state
  • Project blueprints, CAD sketches, dimension details, and design preferences

3. Use of Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Prepare customized dimensional stone carving estimates and material recommendations.
  • Coordinate artisan consultations, logistics, and site delivery arrangements.
  • Send progress updates and photographs during your piece's hand-carving journey.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or proprietary architectural blueprints with third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement strict administrative and electronic safeguards to ensure all inquiries, phone numbers, and design specifications remain confidential within our atelier workshop team.
