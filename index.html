ABOUT US

Bespoke Stone Artistry from the Heart of Jaipur

In the heart of Rajasthan, Jaipur, we transform raw natural stone into pieces of bespoke art. For 40 years, every single piece that leaves our workshop meets the same standard of quality and precision — combining modern CNC precision with skilled artisans who check every little detail.

From architects and interior designers to hotels and construction companies, we work across a wide range of projects — from sacred temples and royal heritage hotels to contemporary modern homes and corporate offices — delivering high-precision stone work worldwide.

Our Creation Process

1

Connect & Vision

Share your space boundaries, architectural drawings, or reference photos with us over a quick call or WhatsApp.

2

Material & Design

Select your preferred natural stone type, texture, and color. We finalize precise 2D/3D design blueprints together.

3

Carving Process

Our master artisans and high-precision multi-axis CNC machines start transforming raw stone blocks into art.

4

Quality Check

We perform rigorous inspection of every dimension, edge finish, relief depth, and surface polish before dispatch.

5

Delivery & Install

Safe protective packaging, worldwide shipping, and seamless installation guidance at your project site.
