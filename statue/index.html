Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What are the differences between genuine Makrana marble moortis and synthetic cultured marble statues?

Genuine Makrana marble moortis are chiseled from single, natural metamorphic stone blocks quarried in Rajasthan. Makrana marble is composed of 98%+ calcium carbonate, giving it a cool, resonant acoustic tone when tapped, crystalline translucency that allows light to penetrate up to 3mm, and a non-porous surface that absorbs neither water nor abhishek liquids. In sharp contrast, synthetic cultured marble and polyresin statues are cast from liquid polymer resin mixed with pulverized marble dust. Over time, resin statues turn dull yellow under sunlight, release chemical VOC odors when subjected to diya heat, and develop micro-cracks that absorb oil and water. For consecration (Prana Pratishtha) in temples and home sanctums, ancient scriptures mandate genuine natural stone.

Q. What Shilpa Shastra rules govern the proportions and facial features of Hindu deity statues?

Shilpa Shastra prescribes strict mathematical canons (Iconometry or Tala Mana) that govern the height, stance (Bhanga), and facial proportions of each deity. For instance, major male deities like Lord Shiva, Vishnu, and Rama follow the Dasa-Tala system (divided into 120 equal angula units), while goddesses often follow the Nava-Tala system. The facial expression (Bhav) must radiate Saumya (peaceful benevolence) with gentle smile (mandahasya), open lotus-petal eyes (padmapatraksha), and symmetrical ears. Mudras (hand gestures such as Abhaya and Varada) must strictly align with the divine attributes. At Shree Ram & Company, our Sompura sculptors hand-measure every curve with traditional calipers to guarantee sacred scriptural fidelity.

Q. How do you care for and clean a handcrafted white marble moorti at home?

Natural Makrana white marble moortis are naturally durable but should be maintained with gentle care. For daily abhishek, use pure fresh water, milk, or curd, and wipe dry immediately with a clean, soft muslin or microfiber cloth to prevent water spot mineralization. Never apply chemical detergents, bleaching agents, acid, or harsh scrubbers, as these erode the delicate hand-polished luster. For gold leaf ornamentation and delicate natural color pigments, avoid abrasive scrubbing around eyes and jewelry; simply dab gently with a damp cotton ball. Shree Ram & Company provides protective breathable sealants that preserve the stone's radiance for generations.

Q. How are delicate monolithic marble statues packaged and insured for domestic and international transit?

Monolithic marble statues feature delicate protrusions like crowns, fingers, weapons, and halos that require specialized export-grade crating. At our Jaipur workshop, each moorti is cocooned in multiple layers of shock-absorbing expanded polyethylene (EPE) foam, shrink wrap, and corner edge protectors. The sculpture is then anchored inside a bespoke ISPM-15 heat-treated fumigated wooden crate with internal wooden brace struts and high-density foam buffers that prevent any movement during shipment. We arrange door-to-door transit across all Indian metro cities and worldwide international sea/air freight with 100% comprehensive marine transit insurance.
