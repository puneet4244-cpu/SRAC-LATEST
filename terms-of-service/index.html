1. Bespoke Stone Artistry Orders

Each architectural product manufactured by Shree Ram & Company (Vijeta Stone)—including double height wall reliefs, staircase wall cladding, marble mandirs, statues, fountains, and CNC jali panels—is custom-sculpted based on confirmed architectural drawings, site measurements, and chosen natural stones.

2. Natural Stone Characteristics

Natural stones such as Makrana Marble, Dholpur Sandstone, Jaisalmer Yellow, and Italian Travertine possess unique natural veining, mineral deposits, texture variations, and color tones. These natural characteristics are authentic hallmarks of genuine quarried stone and are celebrated as distinctive artisan traits.

3. Quotations, Pricing & Payment

  • All initial estimates are based on provided approximate dimensions and stone specifications.
  • Final pricing is locked upon approval of confirmed 2D/3D shop drawings.
  • Custom fabrication orders require a scheduled milestone advance to begin raw block procurement and hand-carving.

4. Packaging, Transit & Pan-India Delivery

All finished stone pieces are packed inside heavy-duty fumigated wooden crates with high-density thermocol and foam cushioning to ensure 100% damage-free road transport across India and overseas ports. On-site unloading and installation support can be arranged with our master craftsmen upon request.
